Roster · Hero shards ◆

Bosses drop hero shards (elite fights sometimes do). Spend them to rank up a hero's skill or unlock new heroes. Skills, unlocked heroes and your party all stay when you retire. Tap Swap in on a hero, then choose which party slot they replace.
Your deck holds up to 10 cards and each card can be played once. Every card returns when you beat a boss or die. Buy common and uncommon tiles; rare, epic and legendary tiles drop only from bosses. Burn weak cards so better ones come up (deck keeps at least 5). Echo-glyph cards go back into the draw pile when played.
Relics drop from fights (Ruins and Battlefield most often), boss kills and Relic Vaults. Duplicates stack.

How to play: drag a tile card from your hand onto a tile on the road (or tap the card, then tap the tile) to replace it. Cards are single-use until the next boss or a wipe. Fights pay far more gold than rest tiles and build Battle Fervor: +20% gold per consecutive fight (up to +100%), lost when you rest at a Village or Springs or fall. Chain fights, then rest. Tap a hero icon under the battle to level them up; open Company to reorder the formation. Neighbouring tiles can make combos (gold chip under the tile). Select a card and tap Inscribe to etch a permanent glyph onto it.
