Formation: the top hero is the front line. They stand nearest the enemy and take about half of all attacks. Use ▲ ▼ to reorder. The hero strip under the battle mirrors the battlefield, front line on the right. Tap a hero icon under the battle to level them up.

Roster · Hero shards

Bosses drop hero shards (elite fights sometimes do). Every shard belongs to a random hero, and rarer heroes drop less often. Collect enough of a hero's own shards to unlock them (once you reach their act) and to rank up their skill. Skills, unlocked heroes and your party all stay when you retire. Tap Swap in on a hero, then choose which party slot they replace.
You can keep up to 10 tiles, and each tile is laid once before it comes round again. Every tile returns when you beat a boss or die. Buy common and uncommon tiles; rare, epic and legendary tiles drop only from bosses. Burn weak tiles so better ones come up (keep at least 5). Echo-glyph tiles go back into the pile when laid.
Relics drop from fights (Ruins and Battlefield most often), boss kills and Relic Vaults. Duplicates stack.

How to play: drag a tile card from your hand onto a tile on the road (or tap the card, then tap the tile) to replace it. Cards are single-use until the next boss or a wipe. Fights pay far more gold than rest tiles and build Battle Fervor: +20% gold per consecutive fight (up to +100%), lost when you rest at a Village or Springs or fall. Chain fights, then rest. Tap a hero icon under the battle to level them up; open Company to reorder the formation. Neighbouring tiles can make combos (gold chip under the tile). Select a card and tap Inscribe to etch a permanent glyph onto it.
